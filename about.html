01 — COMPANY PROFILE

Built around one problem: corrosive chemicals

Our equipment is sold under the brand name “Parth”. It covers spiral chemical storage tanks, reaction vessels, PPFRP storage tanks, blowers and scrubber systems.

Alongside that we make air pollution control systems — scrubbers, venturis, blowers, pickling tanks, reaction vessels with stirrer — plus HDPE road tankers, pipe fittings and ball, foot and NRV valves.
